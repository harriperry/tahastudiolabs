TAHA Studio Labs ScriptEngine

Type the idea. Get the whole production. ScriptEngine turns one story idea into a full cinematic production script, scene by scene, with dialogue, camera direction and sound effects.

What ScriptEngine does

  • Writes scene-by-scene scripts with dialogue, camera direction and SFX.
  • Keeps a character registry so every character stays consistent across scenes.
  • Helps with character avatars and thumbnail prompts for your finished video.
  • Generates Facebook posts for your video.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an API key?

Yes. You bring your own Anthropic API key, and everything else stays in your browser.

Who is ScriptEngine for?

Video creators, storytellers and producers who want a complete script for a short film, documentary or series episode from a single idea.

How does it relate to ScriptForge?

ScriptEngine writes the story. ScriptForge turns a script into timed segments for AI video generators.
