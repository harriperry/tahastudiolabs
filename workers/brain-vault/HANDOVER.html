V2 Part E: client video uploads

  • Video clips (Videoklipp) in the client portal's Uploads step: MP4 or MOV, at most 60 seconds and 200 MB per clip, up to 10 clips. Too long, too big or the wrong type gets a message in Swedish and English.
  • Sent straight from the phone to storage in 5 MB parts with a progress bar. If the connection drops, it waits and carries on by itself. If the page is closed, the portal shows the unfinished upload and continues from where it stopped when she picks the same file again. Unfinished uploads are cleared after two days.
  • The phone measures the clip before sending; the Vault reads the length from the finished file's header as well, so a 61 second clip is refused even when the phone cannot tell. No other processing on the server.
  • Your side: a Video clips box in the client's Intake tab with name, size, length and date. Click to download and edit in your own tools.
  • The Business Brain gets the clips' file names only; the clips are never sent to an AI provider.
  • GDPR: consent moves to c-5 (one new point about video clips, and "short video clips" in What we collect), and privacy.html section 12 has a new paragraph in both languages. Existing clients confirm once on their next visit, and cannot upload videos before they do. Export lists the clips with their length; erasure removes them from storage.

Live since 7 October 2026. Migration 11 is applied; all 6 existing client files were kept (same count and total size before and after). Gate: a client uploads a 60 second phone video with the progress bar, it survives a dropped connection, a 61 second or 201 MB file is refused with a bilingual message, and you download the clip from the Intake tab.

V2 Part D: results that feed the next campaign

  • Business type per client in the Monthly plan box: Restaurant or cafe, Salon or barber, or Other. It picks her results questions. New clients start as Other; set Chef Sandy to Restaurant or cafe.

Every answer is optional, she can skip, and she can change her numbers later. Number keyboard on phones, Swedish and English. It is not asked once she answered or skipped, or once you filled the numbers in yourself.
  • You get one email when she reports (company and month only, no numbers).

  • GDPR: the business type and every answer (hers and your corrections) are in the client's export and erased with the client. The free text asks clients not to write customers' names. No consent change: the client is reporting her own business numbers.
