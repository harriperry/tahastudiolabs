V2 phase G2b: landing pages, tracking and enquiries

  • A Landing page box under the Visual Pack on every saved campaign: the address (tahastudiolabs.com/go/<client>/<campaign>), how customers reach the business (WhatsApp, phone, booking or order link, address, hours), the offer's end date, which of the client's own photos and real reviews to show, and the enquiry form on or off.
  • Publish builds the page in your browser from assets/growth/landing.template.html, makes the images (WebP in two widths, plus a 1200 x 630 link preview), uploads them and sends the finished HTML to the Vault. Updates keep the same link. Preview shows it at phone and desktop width and the ended message. Take down answers 410.
  • Download as HTML gives one self-contained file (images embedded, under 3 MB) and a bilingual guide for WordPress, Wix and Squarespace. Its buttons go through tracked short links and its form posts with the page's token. Copy embed code gives an iframe for site builders.
  • Tracked links and offer codes: one link per post, ad, bio, the video and two QR codes (flyer, counter), each with its own offer code (SANDY-IG, SANDY-FB...). Copy link, Copy text with link, QR as PNG or SVG. Codes never change once made.
  • Performance box: visits, visitors, clicks, QR scans and enquiries per channel; before and after numbers; fee, ad spend and average order value; cost per enquiry, cost per new customer, estimated return and best channel, as in spec 9.4. Your own signed-in visits are never counted.
  • Portal: a Your campaign page card on the status screen, Your enquiries (mark contacted, delete, CSV) and a printable Campaign report. Enquiry emails carry no content.
  • Counting is cookieless: no IP address stored, a daily salted hash for unique visitors, raw events deleted after 90 days, enquiries 90 days after the offer ends, pages taken down 30 days after it. Mark as left takes every page down at once; erasure removes pages, links, events, totals, enquiries and costs.
  • Consent moves to c-3 (one new point about campaign pages and enquiries), and privacy.html section 12 has a new paragraph in both languages. Existing clients confirm once on their next visit.
  • Part H hook: every campaign text reaches the page through campaignText() in assets/growth-landing.js. Part H only changes that function to read approved Swedish text.

V2 phase G1: Visual Pack and Grok removal

  • Make visuals is step 2 of the Campaign Generator (on by default). Add visuals does the same for a campaign made before Part G. One call through ScriptForge's relay with Harry's key, checked against the campaign-2 schema and the house rules, one automatic retry.
  • Every output that needs an image gets a brief at its platform size from assets/growth/platforms.json: offer, video cover (plus a TikTok cover when TikTok is a channel), each social post, five ads, landing page hero and link preview, email header, Google Business post.
  • House rules in the prompt and in code: a dish, the place or the team is always the client's own photo, or shows Photo needed from client with what to ask for; no text inside any image (overlay text separate, in the campaign language); people cast from the brain's audience; colours from the brand kit; prompts in English; alt text in Swedish and English.
  • Each brief card: Copy prompt (or Copy photo request), Copy overlay, Edit, the size, the clear zone, and Attach finished image with a Made with AI tick. Images go to R2 under the client's folder with the ai_image flag and are listed on the card and in the Visual Pack strip. Clients see them in Part B.
  • Brand kit per client: Import brand.json from D:\BRAND-KITS\<brand>\brand.json, or type the colours.
  • Send to ScriptForge has two targets, Veo 3.1 and HeyGen. The formatted segments start on the chosen generator.
  • Grok removed from ScriptForge (generator option, xAI key field, relays, prompt branches, page copy, FAQ, privacy policy, terms, llms.txt) and from ScriptEngine (provider switch, key field, relay). A saved xAI key in ScriptForge is deleted and that person sees a one-time notice that segments now use Veo 3.1. A saved Grok provider in ScriptEngine moves to Anthropic with a one-time notice.
