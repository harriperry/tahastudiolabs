V2 phase G1: Visual Pack and Grok removal

  • Make visuals is step 2 of the Campaign Generator (on by default). Add visuals does the same for a campaign made before Part G. One call through ScriptForge's relay with Harry's key, checked against the campaign-2 schema and the house rules, one automatic retry.
  • Every output that needs an image gets a brief at its platform size from assets/growth/platforms.json: offer, video cover (plus a TikTok cover when TikTok is a channel), each social post, five ads, landing page hero and link preview, email header, Google Business post.
  • House rules in the prompt and in code: a dish, the place or the team is always the client's own photo, or shows Photo needed from client with what to ask for; no text inside any image (overlay text separate, in the campaign language); people cast from the brain's audience; colours from the brand kit; prompts in English; alt text in Swedish and English.
  • Each brief card: Copy prompt (or Copy photo request), Copy overlay, Edit, the size, the clear zone, and Attach finished image with a Made with AI tick. Images go to R2 under the client's folder with the ai_image flag and are listed on the card and in the Visual Pack strip. Clients see them in Part B.
  • Brand kit per client: Import brand.json from D:\BRAND-KITS\<brand>\brand.json, or type the colours.
  • Send to ScriptForge has two targets, Veo 3.1 and HeyGen. The formatted segments start on the chosen generator.
  • Grok removed from ScriptForge (generator option, xAI key field, relays, prompt branches, page copy, FAQ, privacy policy, terms, llms.txt) and from ScriptEngine (provider switch, key field, relay). A saved xAI key in ScriptForge is deleted and that person sees a one-time notice that segments now use Veo 3.1. A saved Grok provider in ScriptEngine moves to Anthropic with a one-time notice.
